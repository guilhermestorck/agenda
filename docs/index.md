# agenda

A GTK4/libadwaita calendar for Linux, with multi-account Google Calendar sync. Free and
open source under the MIT licence.

agenda is desktop software. It runs entirely on your own computer, stores your calendar in a
local database, and talks to no server other than Google's own Calendar API. There is no
agenda service, no account to create, and no backend that ever sees your data.
